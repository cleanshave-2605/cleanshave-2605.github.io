Course project, GNR638 | Prof. Biplab Bannerjee | March–April 2026.

- Addressed information flow obstruction, lossy projection, and suboptimal normalisation in ResNet-50.
- Implemented iResNet-50 from scratch and trained it for 100 epochs to overcome ResNet-50 bottlenecks.
- Achieved 85.31% Top-1 accuracy using a 25.56 million parameter iResNet-50 on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100.
