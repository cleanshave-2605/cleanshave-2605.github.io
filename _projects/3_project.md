Course project, CS6103 | Prof. Arpit Agarwal | February–May 2025.

- Uncovered a flaw in standard RLHF due to the assumption of perfectly homogeneous preferences across all users.
- Engineered personalised preference models across 40 workers and two distinct clusters using vector embeddings.
- Achieved 53% and 52.7% win rates for the two user clusters, compared with a 52% baseline.
