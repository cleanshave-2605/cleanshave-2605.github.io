Course project, CS 747 | Prof. Shivaram Kalyankrishnan | November–December 2025.

- Devised a Microchess agent using reinforcement learning to compete against random and rational opponents.
- Engineered Negamax with Alpha-Beta pruning and Quiescence search to model gameplay.
- Achieved 94% and 84% win rates against random and rational opponents respectively across 100 games.
